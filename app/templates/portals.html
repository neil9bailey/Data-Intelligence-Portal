How Users Should Work This

Before an opportunity lands: add the buyer portal instance, confirm registration, and capture owner/MFA notes.

When a notice appears: create a retrieval task linked to the matching portal and opportunity.

After human download: add the document on the opportunity documents page and paste permitted requirement text for extraction.

After extraction: review requirements and quality questions before including them in reports.
