Portal Rule

Delta, In-Tend, JAGGAER and ProContract are treated as account-required for full tender pack retrieval by default. DIP stores account references and status only, never passwords.

Automation Rule

Automated retrieval is read-only and only runs against approved public/API endpoints. Anything requiring login/MFA becomes a human retrieval task.

Output Rule

Reports can run enabled connectors first, but retrieved information remains pending human review until accepted by the team.
